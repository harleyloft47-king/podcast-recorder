Use headphones while this is on. Through speakers, the mic will pick itself up and squeal.

Soundboard

Click a pad or press its number to play; click again to stop. Sounds play into the recording. Add sound puts your own music or effect on a new pad; ✎ Edit renames, replaces or deletes a pad. Wear headphones so the mic doesn't pick the sound up a second time.
